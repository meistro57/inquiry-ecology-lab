Inquiry Ecology Lab

Two AI personas, a bridge, and the question of whether their conversations generate instruments — not just ideas.

pairing → domain starter → stance interaction → roles

The thesis

The research unit moved from persona to inquiry ecology. Two personas with fixed invariants form a pairing whose geometry sets the domain of discussion, while the starter sets the stance and the interaction assigns roles. The same persona can be a "generator" in one pairing and a "limiter" in another — roles are verbs, not nouns.

Memory changes trajectory, not geometry

The deep attractor re-derives from scratch; the distinctions are memory-carried and die under an amnesia-strip.

Convergence ≠ synthesis

Every run converges (an architectural prior). What matters is what the convergence did — preserved, reconciled, or papered over.

The organism was the pond

Memory can be external but constitutive. The session is stateless; the ecology is what accumulates.

The concept web

Every concept is a node, scored on four axes — coherence, relational, viability, living — with provenance. Edges are typed: extends, supports, refines, guards. Hover a node to read it.

node size = viability score · verified nodes glow · the web is the archive's retrieve-path

Inquiry health ≠ inquiry correctness

The governing discovery: a conversation can show beautiful internal telemetry — balanced operators, preserved anomalies, corrigibility — while never meeting consequence. So the lab tracks two layers at two tempos.
