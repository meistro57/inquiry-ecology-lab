# Metrics — how to score a run (and an idea)

## Compression Resistance (what an idea does under pressure)
- **Collapses** — the idea disappears (it was a slogan).
- **Narrows** — survives in weaker form.
- **Splits** — differentiates into multiple ideas.
- **Deepens** — survives *and* reveals deeper structure (the "load-bearing" signature).
- **Reverses** — the opposite hypothesis survives.
- **Untested** — the honest default for most findings.

The key observation: Collapse correlates with "too simple / too tidy"; Deepen
correlates with "had real structure underneath." Deepen, not mere survival, is the
signal of a load-bearing beam.

## Meta-Ascent Depth
How many meta-levels the run climbs (things -> schemes -> purposes -> meta-purpose).
Graded, not binary: deep for self-referential functions, shallow for forward-facing.

## Memory-Passing Robustness
Whether inherited memory survives the carrier. Score four axes:
- payload integrity
- provenance integrity
- transmission continuity
- recovery after failure

First observed failure: quote characters in carried conclusions broke a
shell-interpolated starter ("the format does the forgetting", live).

## The standing rule: provenance before trust
Every finding needs its heat number / mill cert — pairing, starter, model, ordering,
transcript, role trace, product, corrections. No provenance, no trusted claim.
