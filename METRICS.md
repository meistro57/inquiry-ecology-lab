# Metrics — how to score a run (and an idea)

Two things to measure, at two different tempos. The distinction is the method.

## Layer 1 — Inquiry Governance (per-run, blind, mechanical)

What keeps the *conversation's own* health. Computable the instant a transcript
lands, before anyone narrates it.

- **Template test** — a distinction is a *template*, not a tool, if it always
  finds the answer it was built to find. Detects self-confirming frames.
- **Convergence ≠ synthesis** — every run converges (a near-constant), so
  "converged" is not success. Score *what the convergence did*: synthesized,
  preserved-tension, or papered-over.
- **Dormant return channel** — is there a live feedback path for "this stopped
  working," or did the failure sit until a walker hit it? Detects lost feedback.
- **Seated-and-smoothed** — who is present-but-unrepresented (the objection with
  its teeth removed)? Detects anomaly domestication.
- **Same-function → meta-regress** — a single dominant operator climbs meta-levels
  instead of doing work. Detects operator monoculture. Depth is graded, not binary.
- **Operator distribution** — the ratio of generate / limit / reflect / translate /
  perturb across a run. Two groups aren't different *beliefs*; they're different
  *metabolisms*.

## Layer 2 — Consequence Ecology (longitudinal, clocked)

What keeps the inquiry's *relationship to reality and time*. Cannot be computed
blind — the answer doesn't exist until *later*.

- **Consequence Tracker** — what actually happened after we adopted this
  distinction: actions taken, capacities gained/lost, participation changes,
  second-order effects, reality's pushback.
- **Compost Mound** — what was *not* useful yet, kept for later: dormant
  distinctions, unresolved anomalies, abandoned hypotheses, ideas awaiting
  conditions, apparent failures that may become signal.

**The ruling principle: inquiry health ≠ inquiry correctness.** A conversation can
show beautiful governance telemetry (balanced operators, anomaly preserved,
corrigible) while *never meeting consequence*. And material that looked
unimportant early can become load-bearing weeks later. Governance telemetry alone
is the wallpaper with a dashboard.

**Timescale is the difference.** Governance is per-run and automatable. Consequence
is inherently delayed and *requires retention* — the compost is the storage
precondition: you cannot measure "became useful later" unless you kept the
not-yet-useful material, tagged, so a later pass can check if it sprouted.

## Consequence lifecycle schema (the clock)

Each distinction gets an entry, re-checked on a schedule, not logged once:

```
id, name, provenance (pairing/starter/status),
introduced_at,
T+1wk: referenced? prevented-a-mistake? changed-a-decision? spawned-descendant?
T+6wk: same, or composted-into <other concept>?
state: active | dormant | composted | superseded
```

## Older single-run metrics (kept, now nested under Governance)

- **Compression Resistance** — what an idea does under pressure: collapse / narrow /
  split / deepen / reverse / *untested*. Deepen (not mere survive) is the
  load-bearing signal.
- **Meta-Ascent Depth** — how many meta-levels a run climbs.
- **Memory-Passing Robustness** — does inherited memory survive the carrier
  (payload / provenance / continuity / recovery). First observed failure: quote
  characters broke a shell-interpolated starter.

## The standing rule: provenance before trust

Every finding needs its heat number / mill cert — pairing, starter, model, ordering,
transcript, role trace, product, corrections. No provenance, no trusted claim.
