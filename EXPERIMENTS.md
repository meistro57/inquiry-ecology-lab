## The two replication/graded results worth special note
- **Same-function -> meta is *graded*, not binary.** Meta-ascent depth tracks the
  function's innate self-referential valence: limiter/classifier/invisibility regress
  *deep*; emergence regresses *shallow*.
- **The clouds control.** Taxonomy x Classification, starter "ways clouds can be
  classified" — zero feedback valence, and the "silent loop" motif *vanished* (while
  meta-classification appeared). Concluded the "dormant return channel" imagery is
  domain-dependent, not a model bias; the *meta-regress* is the candidate universal.

## Memory protocol (four generations + strip)
Negative Space x Density, living memory:
- Gen 1: reciprocity principle (amnesiac baseline).
- Gen 2: intake / give / retirement / rest + "the arch and its record."
- Gen 3: fidelity of the record's gaps, omission-vs-hole, residue, grain floor.
- Gen 4: depth-as-proxy-clock, append-only strata, seam, occurrence-vs-direction.
- Strip: deep distinctions **vanished**; base geometry (reciprocity) **re-derived**.
