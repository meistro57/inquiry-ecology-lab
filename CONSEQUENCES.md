# Consequence Tracker

The ledger that METRICS.md's "Consequence lifecycle schema" specifies. This is where
*what actually happened after* a distinction/decision gets observed and recorded — the
column that, until now, has been **empty by design** (see RETROSPECTIVE.md, Finding 2:
"consequence is retro-unscorable, and that is the finding").

Entries here are what fill that column. The schema (from METRICS.md) is:
`id, name, provenance, introduced_at, T+1wk, T+6wk, state` — re-checked on a schedule,
not logged once.

## C-001 — Decision #001 landed; and independently reproduced a distinction from extraction × optimization

- **id:** C-001
- **type:** decision-consequence (the observed effect of a *decision*, not a *distinction*)
- **provenance:** Decision #001 (DECISIONS.md); resonance with run **extraction ×
  optimization** (Optimization_Agent, round 4: *"name what the verification buys, in units
  of the thing valued, before spending it"*).
- **introduced_at:** 2026-11-11
- **consequence (observed):** `CONTACT-TO-CONSEQUENCE.md` produced on the first pass from
  complete in-chat text; no redo required; momentum preserved; human-ratified.
- **resonance (corroborative, NOT causal):** the decision's rule — *proceed rather than
  block on a non-critical, redundant missing input* — independently reproduces the
  extraction × optimization principle *name what the verification buys before spending it*.
  This is the first time a distinction articulated *inside* a chat-bridge run was later
  observed operating *outside* it, in real agent behavior during a live task.
- **direction note (load-bearing):** the decision was made *before* the resonance was
  noticed; the connection surfaced only via post-hoc reflection (Appalachian's question).
  This is therefore **convergent corroboration, not causal transmission** — a datapoint that
  the run's finding describes *real* behavior, not evidence that the run *caused* the behavior.
- **T+1wk:** open. **Re-check trigger:** re-open if the missing artifact later proves
  load-bearing (if it does, the decision's reasoning would need revisiting).
- **T+6wk:** open.
- **state:** active.

### Why this entry matters (and what it is *not*)

- It is the tracker's **first real (non-simulated) datapoint** — the consequence column is
  no longer empty.
- It is the **smallest kind** of consequence: *corroboration*, not *transformation* — the
  world didn't change because of the decision; rather, a principle the lab derived was
  observed already-running in the lab's own behavior.
- It is **not** a "contact with reality" badge. Consequence ≠ external verification: this
  was still the lab observing itself, now made legible. The badge waits on a consequence
  that a party with no stake in us looking good can see.
